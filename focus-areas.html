Three Areas of Focused Care

Where I Bring Continued Attention

Beyond general acupuncture practice, these are three areas I return to again and again — through ongoing study, and through the patients who trust me with them.

01 — Beauty

Facial Rejuvenation, Naturally

My graduate specialization was in TCM Surgery, with extensive focused training in dermatology — a clinical foundation that shapes how I approach facial work. My protocol layers multiple modalities: facial acupuncture for lift and muscle tone, guided manual and lymphatic drainage techniques to reduce puffiness, facial gua sha and cupping to move stagnation and sculpt the contour, microneedling to stimulate collagen renewal, auricular therapy to support the underlying hormonal balance, and light therapy to further encourage skin repair.

The goal isn't simply relaxation. It's replenishing the flow of qi and blood to the face, restoring the kind of luminous, naturally flushed complexion classical medicine calls "peach blossom skin."

Graduate specialization in TCM Dermatology & Surgery, Tianjin University of TCM

02 — Women's Health

Every Stage, Every Shift

A woman's hormonal landscape is never static — it shifts across the menstrual cycle, through the pursuit of pregnancy, across pregnancy and postpartum recovery, and into perimenopause and beyond. I bring both my Western medical training and deep TCM knowledge to meet each of these stages, treating hormonal balance as a whole-body conversation rather than a single isolated symptom.

My clinical focus includes irregular or painful periods, PCOS-related imbalances, and fertility support — working alongside conventional care (including IUI and IVF protocols) to help prepare the body for conception. I also support postpartum recovery and the perimenopausal transition, addressing hot flashes, sleep disruption, and mood shifts as the body recalibrates.

Continued specialized study in women's reproductive health & fertility support

03 — Foundation

The Root Beneath It All

In TCM, the digestive system is considered the root of postnatal vitality. Every treatment plan I build begins here. Poor sleep, low energy, and digestive imbalance rarely stay contained to themselves — left unaddressed, they quietly undermine everything else: the skin's ability to renew, the body's ability to regulate hormones, the nervous system's ability to truly rest.

Addressing digestion, sleep quality, and sustained energy isn't a side note in my practice — it's the foundation every other treatment is built on. No facial protocol or hormonal support plan holds if the body underneath it is running on empty.

A whole-body approach, rooted in digestive and nervous system health
